ℹ️ About TDA Accounting

Your daily companion for tracking spending & income — simple, honest, and built for real life in USD and LBP.

📝
Effortless entries
Log expenses, income, and salary in seconds — pick a type, a category, an amount, done.
📊
Real insights
Dashboards, calendars, and category breakdowns turn raw numbers into a clear picture.
🖨️
Print-ready reports
Generate polished, perfectly-formatted A4 financial statements you can print or export as a PDF.
🔐
Your data, your control
Stay local-only on this device, or sign in to sync your ledger securely across every device you own.
💱
Dual currency
Built from day one to track USD and LBP side by side, the way people actually spend.
✨
Always improving
We're a small team shipping thoughtful updates based on how you actually use the app.

📜 Rules & Terms

Please read before using TDA Accounting.

1. Your data

This app stores the entries you add so you can track your own spending and income. Keep your login details private — anyone with access to your account can view and edit your ledger.

2. Fair use

Use TDA Accounting for personal financial tracking only. Don't attempt to disrupt the service, access other users' data, or misuse the reporting and export tools.

3. No financial advice

Figures, totals, and reports generated here are for personal record-keeping only and are not financial, tax, or legal advice.

4. Changes

These rules may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.
