🛠️ Admin

Admin tools need the admin-actions Supabase Edge Function deployed on your project (see ADMIN-SETUP.md) and cloud sync turned on. They can't run in local-only mode because managing other people's accounts has to happen on a trusted server, never in the browser.

🛠️ Admin — users

View accounts, ban or unban, and open an account to fix its ledger. Every action here runs through your server-side admin-actions function, which checks that you're an approved admin before doing anything — this page has no special power on its own.

🔒 About passwords: Supabase never stores or exposes a password anyone can read back — not you, not an admin, not even Supabase itself — accounts are secured with one-way hashing, so there's no "reveal" button because that data doesn't exist anywhere retrievable. If you need into an account, use 🔓 Log in as user below to open a real session for them without ever touching a password — that's the safer, auditable version of "just show me the password." For lockouts, use Send reset link to email a secure reset, or Set new password to assign one directly.

📤 Backup & restore

Download your data any time, or restore it from a previous backup. A restore never deletes or changes anything already in your account — it only adds items you don't already have.

Export

Transfers are included in the Transactions export — a transfer is just a ledger entry moving money between two of your accounts. The full JSON backup is the one to keep for restoring your account later; it's the only export that includes attached receipts.

Restore

Only full JSON backups exported from this app can be restored. You'll see a preview of what will be added before anything is imported, and it's always safe to import the same backup more than once — matching items are simply skipped.

📜 Rules & Terms

Please read before using TDA Accounting.

1. Your data

This app stores the entries you add so you can track your own spending and income. Keep your login details private — anyone with access to your account can view and edit your ledger.

2. Fair use

Use TDA Accounting for personal financial tracking only. Don't attempt to disrupt the service, access other users' data, or misuse the reporting and export tools.

3. No financial advice

Figures, totals, and reports generated here are for personal record-keeping only and are not financial, tax, or legal advice.

4. Changes

These rules may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.
